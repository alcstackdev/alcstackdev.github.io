Privacy Policy — Waken: Alarm & Morning

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Waken: Alarm & Morning ("Waken", "the App") is an alarm app with wake-up missions, made by AlcStack Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles and the choices you have. Waken has no accounts and shows no ads.

1. What stays on your device

The App does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your alarms (times, labels, repeat days, missions and sounds), settings, wake-up history, streak and your answers to the first-run questions are stored only on your device; we do not receive them. If you add your own alarm sound or wallpaper photo, the file you pick is copied into the App's private storage on your device and is not uploaded.

2. Camera, photos and motion

The camera is used only for wake-up missions that need it: scanning a barcode or QR code, finding an object, photographing the sky and the wake-up selfie. Camera frames are analyzed on your device (on-device barcode scanning, image labeling and face detection on Android; Apple Vision on iPhone) only to check that the mission is done. Mission photos are not saved and not uploaded. Motion and activity data is used only by the step counter mission, is processed on your device and is not uploaded. The App asks for camera and motion access only when you choose a mission that needs it, and you can decline and use the other missions.

3. Alarms and notifications

Alarms and reminders are created on your device. The App does not use a push notification server. Bedtime and streak reminders are optional and can be turned off in Settings. To ring reliably the App may ask for permissions such as notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm display, running after a restart and an exemption from battery optimization (Android). These permissions are used only to ring your alarms, never to collect data about you.

4. Information collected by service providers

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) – anonymous usage events (for example which screen was opened, that an alarm was created or a mission completed), device model, operating system and app version, approximate region and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – crash reports and technical device state, used to fix bugs.
  • RevenueCat – an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status, used to unlock Waken Pro.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play – process payments. We never see your card or payment details.

We do not show ads, do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and do not sell your data.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Analytics, crash and purchase records are kept only as long as needed to understand usage, fix bugs and manage subscriptions. You may ask us to access, correct or delete data linked to you by emailing alcstackdev@gmail.com.

6. Security

Data sent to our service providers is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data on your device lives in the App's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, together with a new date.

9. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: alcstackdev@gmail.com.
