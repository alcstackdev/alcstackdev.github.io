Privacy Policy — Waken: Alarm & Morning

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Waken: Alarm & Morning ("Waken", "the App") is an alarm app with wake-up missions, made by AlcStack Dev ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. Waken has no accounts and shows no ads.

1. Who we are

AlcStack Dev is responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at alcstackdev@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The App does not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your alarms (times, labels, repeat days, missions and sounds), settings, wake-up history, streak and your answers to the first-run questions are stored only on your device. We do not receive them.

If you add your own alarm sound or wallpaper photo, the file you pick is copied into the App's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded.

3. Camera, photos and motion

The camera is used only for wake-up missions that need it: scanning a barcode or QR code, finding an object, photographing the sky and the wake-up selfie. Camera frames are analyzed on your device (on-device barcode scanning, image labeling and face detection on Android; Apple Vision on iPhone) only to check that the mission is done. Mission photos are not saved and not uploaded.

Motion and activity data is used only by the step counter mission to count your steps. It is processed on your device and not uploaded.

The App asks for camera and motion access only when you choose a mission that needs it. You can decline and use the other missions. Waken does not use your microphone, location, contacts, photo library or health data.

4. Permissions the App may request

Android:

  • Notifications, vibration and wake lock: to show and ring your alarms and optional reminders.
  • Exact alarms and full-screen alarm display: so alarms ring on time and open on the lock screen.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep the alarm sound playing while it rings.
  • Run after restart: to restore your alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Battery optimization exemption (optional): so the system does not put the App to sleep before an alarm.
  • Camera and physical activity: only for the missions described above.
  • Internet and network state: to reach the service providers listed below.

iPhone:

  • Notifications and Time Sensitive notifications: to ring alarm reminders and reach you during Focus.
  • Alarms (iOS 26 and newer): to schedule system alarms.
  • Camera and motion: only for the missions described above.
  • Background audio and background refresh: to keep alarms working on iOS 15 to 25.

None of these permissions is used to collect data about you or to track you.

5. Information collected by service providers

  • Firebase Analytics (Google): anonymous usage events (for example which screen was opened, that an alarm was created or a mission completed), device model, operating system and app version, approximate region from the IP address and an app-instance identifier.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): crash reports and technical device state, used to fix bugs.
  • RevenueCat: an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status and purchase history, used to unlock Waken Pro and keep it in sync.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: process payments and subscriptions under their own privacy policies. We never see your card or payment details.

6. Advertising and tracking

We do not show ads, do not use advertising identifiers, do not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and do not sell or share your data for advertising.

7. How we use information

We use the information above only to run the App, unlock and manage Waken Pro, understand how the App is used, find and fix crashes and improve Waken. We do not build advertising profiles. Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the App and on performing our contract with you for purchases.

8. Sharing and international transfers

We share information only with the service providers listed above, who process it for us, and when the law requires it. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries; they apply their own safeguards.

9. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the App. Analytics, crash and purchase records are kept only as long as needed to understand usage, fix bugs, manage subscriptions and meet legal duties.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data linked to you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to receive a copy of your data and to complain to your data protection authority. To use these rights, email alcstackdev@gmail.com and include your app version. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details such as your order number to find the right records.

11. Security

Data sent to our service providers is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data on your device lives in the App's private storage, protected by your phone's operating system. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page together with a new date. If a change is significant we will tell you in the App.

14. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy: alcstackdev@gmail.com.
