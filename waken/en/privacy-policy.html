Privacy Policy — Waken: Alarm & Morning

Last updated: 2026-10-14

AlcStack Dev ("we", "us") operates the Waken: Alarm & Morning mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address. Your alarms, settings and wake-up history are stored only on your device. Photos taken for photo missions and motion or step data used by the step mission are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. We collect limited technical data: app usage events and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics), device and operating system information, and purchase and subscription status (RevenueCat, Apple App Store, Google Play).

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information used to identify you:

  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google) – usage statistics and crash reports
  • RevenueCat – subscription and purchase management
  • Apple App Store and Google Play – payment processing

Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at alcstackdev@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at alcstackdev@gmail.com.
